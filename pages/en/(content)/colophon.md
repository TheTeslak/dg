Built on **Vue + SSG,** hosted on **Netlify**

Forked [antfu](https://github.com/antfu/antfu.me) and pushed hundreds of changes: new components, i18n, tooltips inspired by [LessWrong](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/bJ2haLkcGeLtTWaD5/welcome-to-lesswrong), [a11y]{term="a11y" definition="An approach to building websites accessible to people with visual, hearing, or motor impairments"}, [JSON-LD]{term="json-ld" definition="Invisible markup for search engines—Google displays rich cards instead of plain links"}, [microformats2]{term="microformats2" definition="HTML classes that help other services understand site structure: identifying the author, date, and article text"}, ✨ visuals, a revamped TOC, mobile version, and bug fixes

⚰️ If [teslak.me](https://teslak.me/) ever goes dark, check [teslak.netlify.app](https://teslak.netlify.app/) or [GitHub](https://github.com/TheTeslak/dg/tree/main/pages)

Platforms and site builders like [Substack](https://substack.com/) don’t offer enough freedom—I want to do lots of little things my own way (and did, right on this site)

Later, I plan to add navigation inspired by non-linear digital gardens, an ✉️ email newsletter, micro-animations, hotkeys, webmentions, and migrate to [Astro](https://astro.build/)
