Bots on the internet look predictable: _First Last_ with no avatar, a female name with an erotic avatar, asjfds5431, or Ivan the Marketer

My profile looks nothing like that. In comments and chats I write in lowercase and abbreviate words in unusual ways; neural networks don't talk like that

Nevertheless, I've been suspected of being non-human dozens of times (because of the robot avatar?), and occasionally even banned

The biggest surprise was [Vastrik.Club]{term="Vastrik.Club" definition="A closed paid community with an IT slant"}: to join, I wrote about myself in detail and informally, and… my application was rejected. I rewrote it with more detail and new links — rejected again!

So (((some))) IT folks couldn't tell a human from a bot. Meanwhile, the Club is full of nameless accounts, as a moderator confirmed. [I cringed and didn't bother rewriting it a third time]{.muted}

After that, being accused of using em dashes doesn't shock me as much anymore
