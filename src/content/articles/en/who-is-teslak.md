A believer in depth, 🔍 research, working with data, and best practices

Early on, I tried my hand at programming: C++ and web dev. Eventually, I moved away from code to the intersection of visuals, technology, and product logic

For a while, I was into illustration and graphic design, but I always felt more at home on the technical side. I get along well with developers, understand how modern systems work, and know the technical constraints of bringing a product to life

I've worked on a [Fintech CRM]{term="Fintech CRM" definition="A system for online brokers and financial platforms — companies through which people trade or invest. I know what good processes look like and value the details

Since the rise of neural networks, I've been actively following _all_ of them, vibe-coding, and understanding where they fall short

I have plenty of gripes with Telegram. That, combined with a desire to connect with the English-speaking global community and a craving for digital independence, led me to build this website

## Nickname

In 2018, I decided to come up with a decent handle

My last name is Iskra — “spark” in Russian

According to the Russian [Wikipedia](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D0%9C%D0%BE%D0%BB%D0%BD%D0%B8%D1%8F), lightning is an electric spark discharge in the atmosphere (in the English wiki, the spark connection is mentioned [elsewhere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electric_spark))

Lightning and electricity naturally lead to Nikola Tesla

And that's how the name came to be. ⚡️

[Not that I'm a fan of Nikola: he was a peculiar, speculative, and slightly eccentric person. Even more so, I'm not a fan of Tesla Inc., though they do make nice cars]{.muted}

Now, I use Teslak as my handle everywhere

The "k" at the end doesn't mean much. Perhaps I was influenced by [KDE Plasma](https://kde.org/) at the time — after all, lightning is plasma, too

## Avatars

I used to use various avatars — especially knights — and for privacy, never real photos. Once I adopted the name Teslak, I switched to a lightning bolt

![A chronological row of Teslak avatars changing from a simple lightning symbol to illustrated characters](/images/avatars.avif "Evolution of the Teslak avatar")

Wanting a more distinct identity, I designed a character, hired an illustrator, and touched up the final design. When I got tired of it, I hired another artist and did even more edits myself; that elf ended up sticking around longer

With the release of Midjourney v4, I generated a robot avatar with a nod to [Master Chief](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_Chief_(Halo)>). With v5, I made another one, which is the one I use today
