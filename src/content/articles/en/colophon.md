This site is built with **Astro as a static site** and hosted on **Netlify**

I forked [antfu](https://github.com/antfu/antfu.me) and made hundreds of changes: new components, multilingual support, tooltips inspired by [LessWrong](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/bJ2haLkcGeLtTWaD5/welcome-to-lesswrong), [a11y]{term="a11y" definition="An approach to building websites that are usable by people with visual, hearing, or motor impairments"}, [JSON-LD]{term="json-ld" definition="Invisible markup for search engines — Google shows rich cards instead of plain links"}, [microformats2]{term="microformats2" definition="HTML classes that help other services understand the structure of a site: where the author, date, and article text are"}, ✨ visuals, a different table of contents, a mobile version, and bug fixes

⚰️ If [teslak.me](https://teslak.me/) ever stops opening, check [teslak.netlify.app](https://teslak.netlify.app/) or [GitHub](https://github.com/TheTeslak/dg/tree/main/pages)

Site builders and platforms like [Substack](https://substack.com/) don't give enough freedom — there are many little things I want to do differently (and did, on this site).

Next I plan to add navigation in the spirit of non-linear digital gardens, an ✉️ email newsletter, more micro-animations, hotkeys, and webmentions

By the way, you can change the article background by pressing the up arrow
